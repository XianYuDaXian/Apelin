Apelin 0.2.9 is made for iPhone Duo.

- On iPhone Duo Apelin sat in the middle of the screen with its bars across the top. It is now built against the iOS 27.1 SDK: it fills the display, the page's buttons run down the side under the status bar, and the conversation, the message box and the button that jumps to the latest message keep to the space the side bar leaves.
- On the inner display held upright, the list of chats took so much room beside the conversation that code was cut off. When there is no room for both, the list opens as a drawer; held sideways, and on iPad, it stays beside the conversation.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
