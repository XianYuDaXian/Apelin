Apelin 0.2.10 asks, after you have built with it for a while, whether you would star it on GitHub.

- Once you sign in with GitHub, the tenth build that finishes asks whether you like Apelin. Star Now stars Apelin on GitHub with your account and it never asks again; Maybe Later asks again ten builds later, three times at most. If your account has starred Apelin already, it never asks.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
