Apelin 0.3.2 publishes the release builds you made, keeps small images small in a conversation and gives a new Settings pane an icon.

- Asked to publish two release builds to your package repository, the agent refused both, calling them release builds and asking for release builds in the same message. Release builds are now published as they were built, roothide and rootless together.
- A small image, such as an icon, filled a large part of the conversation and looked blurry. A small image is now shown at its own size, never under 32 points, so it stays small and sharp; photos and screenshots fill the space as they did, and a tap still opens the original.
- A Settings pane made from the Preference Bundle template showed its name in Settings with no icon. New panes now carry an icon, at every screen scale, which you can replace with your own.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
